Privacy Policy — Badator!

Last updated: 2026-11-23
Controller: Dev Igoni — Iker Goñi Cuesta (Pamplona, España)
Contact: dev.mail.igoni@gmail.com

Badator! provides local bus arrival information, nearby stop discovery, favorites and one-time alerts for Donostia / San Sebastián. It is independent and unofficial.

Location: when you request nearby stops and grant approximate or precise location permission, the app uses your location to identify nearby stops. Location is optional, used ephemerally in memory on the device to sort nearby stops, and is never persisted or transmitted to third parties.

On-device data: favorites, recent stops, settings and configured alerts are stored locally to provide app features. Data is stored locally in Room (SQLite) and DataStore with android:allowBackup="false" (no automatic Google Drive backup). You can delete it in-app, via Android app settings, or by uninstalling.

External arrival data source: the app contacts an external transit API. The provider may receive your IP address, connection metadata and requested stop identifiers. The public Dbus API (http://app.dbus.eus/SSIIMovilWSv2/ws/cons/tiemposParada) currently responds only over HTTP. The manifest declares cleartextTrafficPermitted="true" exclusively for app.dbus.eus, and no other network endpoints are used. Accordingly, requests to that endpoint are not encrypted in transit.

Analytics and advertising: Badator! does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or telemetry SDKs. Its dependencies are strictly functional (AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Room, OkHttp, kotlinx.serialization, and Kotlin Coroutines). It does not use Firebase, Google Play Services, or third-party trackers.

Retention and deletion: Local data (favorites, recent stops, alerts, and preferences) is stored in Room (SQLite) and DataStore inside the app private storage on your device. The app has no user accounts. The manifest sets android:allowBackup="false", so no automatic Google Drive backup is performed. You can delete your data from within the app, by clearing app data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Your rights: contact dev.mail.igoni@gmail.com regarding privacy requests. Controller: Iker Goñi Cuesta (Pamplona, España). Contact: dev.mail.igoni@gmail.com.

Changes: updates to this policy will be posted at https://ikergoni.github.io/bus-dbus/privacy/privacy-policy-en.html.

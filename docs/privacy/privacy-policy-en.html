Privacy Policy — Badator!

Last updated: 2026-11-23
Controller: Dev Igoni — Iker Goñi Cuesta (Pamplona, España)
Contact: dev.mail.igoni@gmail.com

1. What the app does

Badator! lets you check real-time bus arrival times in Donostia / San Sebastián, save favorite stops and lines, find nearby stops, and set arrival alerts. It is an independent and unofficial app, not affiliated with Dbus or the Donostia / San Sebastián City Council.

2. On-device data and permissions

  • Location (optional): ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION are requested only when you explicitly ask to find nearby stops. Your location is processed ephemerally in memory on the device to sort stops by distance; it is never saved to storage and never transmitted to the developer or third parties. The app works fully without location permission.
  • Locally stored data: favorites, recent stops, preferences, and configured alerts are stored solely in the app's private on-device storage using Room (local SQLite) and DataStore. The manifest sets android:allowBackup="false", so Android does not automatically back up this data to Google Drive.
  • Notifications and foreground service (optional): POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC are used only when you configure arrival alerts. While alerts are active, a foreground service checks arrivals and displays a persistent notification, stopping automatically once no active alerts remain.

3. Network requests and external data provider

To display live arrivals, Badator! connects directly to the public Dbus (Donostiako Autobusak) API at http://app.dbus.eus/SSIIMovilWSv2/ws/cons/tiemposParada. These requests may expose your device's IP address, standard connection metadata, and requested stop identifiers to Dbus. You can review the provider's privacy policy at https://www.dbus.eus/privacidad/.

The Dbus API currently responds only over HTTP. The app's network security configuration declares cleartextTrafficPermitted="true" exclusively for app.dbus.eus, and no other network endpoints are contacted. Accordingly, requests to that endpoint are not encrypted in transit.

4. Analytics, advertising, and third parties

Badator! does not collect or share personal data with third parties. It includes no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or telemetry SDKs. Its dependencies are strictly technical and functional (AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Room, OkHttp, kotlinx.serialization, and Kotlin Coroutines), with no Firebase and no Google Play Services.

5. Data retention and deletion

Badator! has no user accounts, and the developer does not store personal data on external servers. Local data remains only on your device until you remove favorites, recents, or alerts within the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

6. Your rights

For any privacy inquiry or to exercise your rights under applicable data protection law (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, or objection), contact dev.mail.igoni@gmail.com.

7. Children

Badator! is intended for a general audience (PEGI 3 / Everyone). It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

Relevant updates to this policy will be published at https://igoni.github.io/bus-dbus/privacy-policy-en.html.

Privacy Policy — Badator!

Last updated: 2026-11-23
Controller: Dev iGoni — Iker Goñi (autónomo, España)
Contact: dev.mail.igoni@gmail.com

Badator! provides local bus arrival information, nearby stop discovery, favorites and one-time alerts for Donostia / San Sebastián. It is independent and unofficial.

Location: when you request nearby stops and grant approximate or precise location permission, the app uses your location to identify nearby stops. Confirmed: location is only used locally to sort nearby stops and is never transmitted to third parties.

On-device data: favorites, recent stops, settings and configured alerts are stored locally to provide app features. Confirmed: local data (favorites, recents, alerts, preferences) stored in Room SQLite. User deletes via UI or uninstall removes all. Backups: android:allowBackup="false" in manifest → no automatic Google Drive backup.

External arrival data source: the app contacts an external transit API. The provider may receive your IP address, connection metadata and requested stop identifiers. The source implementation reviewed uses HTTP for these requests, so do not claim all network data is encrypted in transit.

Analytics and advertising: Confirmed: no analytics, ads, crash reporting, or telemetry SDKs. Dependencies: AndroidX, Compose, Room, OkHttp, kotlinx.serialization, coroutines. No Firebase, no Play Services, no third parties collecting data..

Retention and deletion: Local data (favorites, recents, alerts, preferences): Room SQLite in app private directory. Deletion: user removes via UI or uninstalls app. Backups: android:allowBackup="false" in manifest → no automatic Google Drive backup. Updates: Room migrates schemas; data preserved..

Your rights: contact dev.mail.igoni@gmail.com regarding privacy requests. Legal controller: Iker Goñi (autónomo, España). Contact: dev.mail.igoni@gmail.com.

Changes: updates to this policy will be posted at https://ikergoni.github.io/bus-dbus/privacy-policy-en.html.
